GoHighLevel — suggested custom fields for outbound (adjust to your sub-account)
==================================================================================

Contact
  first_name, last_name, email, phone, company_name, job_title

Outbound meta
  icp_segment          (e.g. fm_buyer, trade_owner, property_manager)
  sequence_name      (e.g. skorp_v1_may2026)
  sequence_step      (1, 2, 3)
  last_touch_at      (date)
  reply_class          (positive, neutral, negative, ooo, bounce)

Compliance
  consent_source       (inbound_only | event | partner_referral | list_import_date)
  do_not_contact       (boolean)

Pipeline stages (example — rename to match GHL)
  new_lead → engaged → meeting_booked → qualified → won / lost

Webhook / automation
  Document inbound webhook URLs in each brand’s ops folder — never commit secrets.
