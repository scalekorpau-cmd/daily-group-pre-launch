Legal page templates — important
==================================
These HTML files are drafting aids only. They are NOT legal advice and are NOT tailored to your
exact entities until your lawyer completes them.

Files (clone per brand; replace all [BRACKETS]):
  TERMS_OF_SERVICE_TEMPLATE.html
  PRIVACY_POLICY_TEMPLATE.html
  COOKIES_POLICY_TEMPLATE.html

Templates include sections on GoHighLevel, outbound calling, and AI-assisted calling (plus
lawyer review notes). Manifest placeholders [LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME] and [ABN] supply the operator
line per site (currently set to TradesBook Pty Ltd / 80 640 074 507 across repos — change per
brand if your lawyer advises a different contracting entity).

Suggested workflow
--------------------
1. Confirm which ACN/ABN entity operates each website (may differ per brand; update manifest if so).
2. Send the three filled drafts + your service model to your Australian business lawyer.
3. After approval, publish at fixed URLs (e.g. /terms, /privacy, /cookies) and link in footer.
4. Store signed-off PDFs in: _incoming\legal-signed-pdfs\
